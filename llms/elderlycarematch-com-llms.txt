# Elderly Care Match

> Elderly Care Match helps families find and compare senior care — assisted living, memory care, adult family homes, independent living, and nursing facilities. Listing coverage is concentrated in Washington State, strongest across the Seattle-Tacoma and wider Puget Sound area. Listings are free to browse, many are license-verified, and families never pay a fee.

- [Find Care Centers](https://www.elderlycarematch.com/care-centers): Search senior care facilities by location, care type, price, vacancies, and Medicaid acceptance.
